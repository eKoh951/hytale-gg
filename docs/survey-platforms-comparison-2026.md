## 🏆 TOP RECOMMENDATIONS

### 1. **TALLY** - Best Overall for Research
**Starting Price:** FREE (Pro at $29/month)  
**Free Plan:** ✅ Unlimited forms, responses, and questions  
**User Rating:** 4.8/5 stars (G2)

**What You'll Love:**
- ✅ **Truly unlimited free plan** with advanced features
- ✅ **Conditional logic** and answer piping
- ✅ **Multi-media embeds** (videos, images, audio, Google Maps)
- ✅ **Advanced calculations** for scoring/pricing
- ✅ **Intuitive form editor** with professional customization
- ✅ **Good integrations** (Slack, Airtable, webhooks)
- ✅ **API available** to all users for free
- ✅ **Mobile-responsive** forms

**Limitations:**
- ⚠️ Smaller template library than competitors
- ⚠️ Custom CSS requires paid plan ($29/month)
- ⚠️ Team workspaces require paid plan

**Perfect For:** Your research surveys - unlimited responses for 100+ players

---

### 2. **GOOGLE FORMS** - Best for Simple Research
**Starting Price:** FREE  
**Free Plan:** ✅ Unlimited forms, responses, questions  
**User Rating:** 4.5/5 stars (G2)

**What You'll Love:**
- ✅ **Completely free** with no usage caps
- ✅ **Google Sheets integration** for automatic data collection
- ✅ **Simple interface** - minimal learning curve
- ✅ **Team collaboration** features
- ✅ **Reliable Google infrastructure**
- ✅ **Basic conditional logic** for question branching

**Limitations:**
- ❌ **Very limited customization** and branding
- ❌ **No payment processing** or advanced integrations
- ❌ **Dated interface** lacking modern design
- ❌ **No advanced features** (calculations, answer piping)
- ❌ **Basic analytics** only

**Perfect For:** Quick, no-frills surveys when budget is $0

---

### 3. **JOTFORM** - Best Premium Option
**Starting Price:** FREE (Bronze at $39/month)  
**Free Plan:** 5 forms with 100 monthly submissions  
**User Rating:** 4.7/5 stars (G2)

**What You'll Love:**
- ✅ **800+ survey templates** for quick setup
- ✅ **100+ app integrations** (Google Analytics, PayPal, HubSpot)
- ✅ **Advanced analytics** with visual reports
- ✅ **Payment processing** built-in
- ✅ **Drag-and-drop editor** with pre-formatted elements
- ✅ **SSL protection** and data security
- ✅ **Real-time collaboration** with team members

**Limitations:**
- ⚠️ **Free plan severely limited** (5 forms, 100 submissions)
- ⚠️ **Steep learning curve** for beginners
- ⚠️ **Can crash sometimes** (bugs/glitches)
- ⚠️ **Overwhelming interface** with too many options

**Perfect For:** If you need advanced features and can pay $39/month

## 💰 TYPEFORM PRICING ANALYSIS (2026)

### Current Typeform Plans

| Plan | Price | Responses/Month | Users | Key Features |
|------|-------|------------------|-------|-------------|
| **Basic** | $29/month | 100 | 1 | Unlimited forms, Typeform branding |
| **Plus** | $59/month | 1,000 | 3 | Remove branding, integrations |
| **Business** | $99/month | 10,000 | 5 | Analytics, priority support |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Custom | SSO, HIPAA, dedicated manager |

### Why Typeform is Expensive for Research

**Problems with Typeform for Your Use Case:**
- ❌ **100 responses/month** on Basic plan (you need 100+)
- ❌ **$59/month** for 1,000 responses (overkill for one survey)
- ❌ **Beautiful but expensive** - you're paying for design, not features
- ❌ **No unlimited free tier** like Tally

**Typeform Strengths:**
- ✅ **Best conversational experience** (one question per page)
- ✅ **Excellent design customization**
- ✅ **Good analytics** on paid plans
- ✅ **Strong brand recognition**

**Verdict:** Not cost-effective for research surveys
