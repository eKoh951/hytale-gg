## 🚨 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### Content Mistakes
- ❌ **Only posting survey links** (no value)
- ❌ **Too many hashtags** (looks spammy)
- ❌ **Ignoring replies** (bad for algorithm)
- ❌ **Same message repeatedly** (boring)

### Strategy Mistakes
- ❌ **Expecting immediate results** (takes time)
- ❌ **Following thousands randomly** (looks spammy)
- ❌ **DMing strangers with links** (annoying)
- ❌ **Giving up after 1 week** (need consistency)

### Technical Mistakes
- ❌ **Protected account** (limits reach)
- ❌ **No profile optimization** (low credibility)
- ❌ **Broken survey link** (wasted effort)
- ❌ **No mobile optimization** (50%+ mobile users)

## 📱 MOBILE OPTIMIZATION

### Twitter Mobile Best Practices
- **Short tweets** (under 200 characters)
- **Clear links** (easy to tap)
- **Mobile-friendly images** (vertical format)
- **Emoji use** (breaks up text)

### Survey Mobile Optimization
- **Mobile-first design** (test on phone)
- **Short questions** (easy to read)
- **Large tap targets** (buttons, links)
- **Fast loading** (under 3 seconds)
